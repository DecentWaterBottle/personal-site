ÜFIP (ÜNB-FNB-IT-Plattform) is a critical infrastructure platform jointly developed by 
Amprion and Open Grid Europe (OGE), launched in November 2025. It enables real-time 
coordination between Germany's electricity transmission operators (ÜNB) and gas 
transmission operators (FNB) during supply bottlenecks, allowing gas-critical power 
plants to be rapidly instructed to generate electricity when the gas network comes under 
strain. The platform fulfils regulatory requirements under §§ 12 and 16 of the 
Energiewirtschaftsgesetz (EnWG).

## My role

I joined the project as a full-stack developer during active development, working across 
the backend, database layer, and frontend. I have since been involved in ongoing 
maintenance and extension of the platform within a regulated KRITIS environment.

Due to the sensitive nature of the project, implementation details are under NDA.
